## 2. Data we process

We may process:

- identity and contact information: name, email, and Mexican phone number;
- authentication and security information: a non-reversible password hash,
  session identifiers, expiry, rotation, and revocation;
- financial or patrimonial information: optional monthly salary, expense
  amounts, balances, and simulated settlements, always in MXN centavos;
- group activity: group name and settings, members, roles, invitations, expense
  descriptions, payers, participants, split method, corrections, and dates;
- selected contacts: names and phone numbers you choose to save from your
  address book or enter manually, including people who may not use Vaquita;
- support and privacy information: requests, communications, minimum identity
  verification, and case outcomes; and
- limited technical information: request method and path, duration, errors, and
  technical identifiers needed for security and operations. Contracted
  infrastructure may process IP addresses and network metadata.

The app keeps session tokens, a profile copy, and the last login email in secure
device storage.

We do not request bank credentials, hold funds, or make real transfers. The
provider included in the beta is a simulator. The current repository contains
no advertising or third-party analytics integration.

## 3. Necessary purposes

We use data to:

- create and protect accounts and manage sign-in and sign-out;
- create groups, manage invitations, and show authorized members;
- record expenses, calculate exact centavo splits, and project balances;
- record simulated settlements separately from expense history;
- save contacts you select and, when you request it, match them to accounts;
- prevent abuse, diagnose errors, maintain backups, and restore service; and
- handle support, security incidents, and privacy rights.

We do not use this data for advertising, profile sales, credit scoring, or
automated decisions with legal effects.

## 4. Salary and express consent

Salary is optional and is used only for proportional splits when the feature is
enabled and every participating member authorizes it. Your exact salary is not
shown to other members, although resulting percentages and amounts may allow
them to infer relative salary differences.

Vaquita must obtain your express consent before collecting salary. You may
refuse or revoke it without losing equal splitting. Removing exact salary must
not change historical expenses, so the feature will not be enabled for an
external beta until historical shares are stored immutably.

## 5. Contact access

Address-book access is optional. Android asks for permission only when you
choose to import. The app displays contacts in memory so you can select entries
and sends only selected names and phone numbers to the server. Manual entry is
also available.

By selecting a contact, you confirm that you are authorized to provide those
details to Vaquita for expense and invitation management. You can delete each
saved contact. Before launch, legal review will define how people who do not yet
use Vaquita receive indirect notice.

## 7. Retention, cancellation, and account deletion

We retain account information while the account is active. Saved contacts are
removed when you delete them or when account deletion is executed. Access
sessions last up to 15 minutes and refresh families up to 30 days; related
records are removed no later than 30 days after expiry or revocation.

Server logs are normally kept for 30 days and security-incident extracts for up
to 90 days or a documented legal hold. Encrypted backups have a maximum 35-day
lifetime, and deletion is reapplied after a restore.

Shared expenses and settlements are not individually edited or deleted because
other members need a correct history. On account deletion, we revoke access,
remove private contacts and unnecessary data, and replace your identity in the
shared ledger with a pseudonymous identifier. Minimum financial facts remain
while the group uses the ledger and during the legally approved blocking
period; they are then deleted or irreversibly disassociated.

A documented dispute, legal obligation, or security investigation may extend a
retention period. Blocked data is not used for ordinary product functions.

## 8. ARCO rights, withdrawal, and use limitation

You may request access, rectification, cancellation, or opposition (ARCO
rights), withdraw consent, limit use or disclosure, or request account deletion
by contacting **[PRIVACY EMAIL]**.

Include your name, a reply channel, the right or data involved, and information
that helps locate the account. We verify identity proportionately, preferably
through an authenticated session or a challenge to the registered email/phone;
we do not request government identification unless necessary.

We will communicate our decision within 20 business days after receiving the
request. If approved, we will execute it within the following 15 business days.
Each period may be extended once for an equal period when circumstances justify
it and we notify you. Requests are free except legally permitted reproduction
or delivery costs.

Cancellation may require blocking or be partially denied when necessary to
protect other members' rights, meet an obligation, or preserve responsibility
arising from processing. We will explain such a decision in writing.

## 9. Security and incidents

We apply administrative and technical controls appropriate to risk, including
HTTPS, authorization, server-side token hashing, Android secure storage,
restricted access, and encrypted remote backups. No system is infallible. If a
breach significantly affects your patrimonial or moral rights, we will notify
you immediately so you can take protective action.

## 10. Notice changes

We will publish the date and updated text at **[PUBLIC NOTICE URL]** and notify
you in the app when a change is material or requires new consent. We will not
use data for an incompatible new purpose without updating the notice and
obtaining applicable consent.

## 11. Authority and review

This draft refers to Mexico's current Federal Law on Protection of Personal
Data Held by Private Parties, whose latest reform listed by the Chamber of
Deputies is November 14, 2025. That law names the Secretariat of
Anti-Corruption and Good Government as the federal authority.

Qualified Mexican counsel must review this text before publication. It is not a
certification of compliance.
